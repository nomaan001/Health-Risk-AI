    From raw readings
    to a reason.

    Drag to explore the six stages.

    1

    Data acquisition

    Medical records, lab results and wearable-device readings are collected.

    2

    Preprocessing

    Missing or impossible values become the feature median. Categories are encoded and numbers standardised.

    3

    Feature engineering

    The most relevant medical features are selected and extracted to lift accuracy and cut computation.

    4

    Model development

    Three models are tuned with grid search and cross-validation, then the best is saved with joblib.

    5

    Risk prediction

    Flask validates inputs, applies the saved scaler and returns a probability and a risk level.

    6

    Interpretation

    SHAP shows which factors pushed the score up or down, so people see reasons, not only a label.

    Three models.
    One chosen.

    Logistic Regression

    A transparent baseline. Slightly less accurate, but easy to reason about.

    Selected

    Random Forest

    Best accuracy. Captures non-linear links between BMI, age and glucose, and resists noise and over-fitting.

    Support Vector Machine

    Performs well after scaling, but is more sensitive to parameter choices.

    Each model is judged on accuracy, precision, recall and F1-score. In healthcare, recall on the high-risk class matters most: a missed case could delay a doctor’s visit.

    How probability becomes a risk level

    Low < 0.35
    Moderate 0.35–0.65
    High > 0.65

    Explainable

    SHAP bars sit beside every result, with LIME as a second opinion.

    Validated inputs

    Every field is range-checked, for example age 1 to 120, before it reaches the model.

    Tested

    All 7 functional test cases pass, from valid input and empty fields to saving a record and viewing history.

    Private by design

    Inputs and predictions are stored in MySQL with encryption in mind for health-data standards.
